Application Process

Follow these simple steps to join our innovative community of learners.

1

Research Programs

Explore our programs to find the one that aligns with your academic interests and career goals. Consider factors like curriculum, faculty expertise, and research opportunities.

2

Prepare Required Documents

Gather all necessary documents including academic transcripts, standardized test scores, letters of recommendation, and a personal statement. International students should also prepare language proficiency test results.

3

Complete Online Application

Fill out the online application form with your personal information, academic history, and program preferences. Be sure to review all information for accuracy before submission.

4

Pay Application Fee

Submit the non-refundable application fee to process your application. Fee waivers may be available for eligible applicants based on financial need.

5

Interview (if required)

Some programs may require an interview as part of the selection process. This can be conducted in-person, via video conference, or by telephone depending on your location.

6

Receive Admission Decision

Admission decisions are typically released within 4-6 weeks after the application deadline. You will be notified via email and through your applicant portal.

Admission Requirements

Review our requirements to ensure your application is complete and competitive.

Undergraduate Requirements

For first-year and transfer students seeking bachelor's degrees.

Completed high school or equivalent

Minimum GPA of 3.0 (on 4.0 scale)

SAT or ACT scores (optional for 2026 entry)

Personal statement (500-650 words)

Two letters of recommendation

Official high school transcripts

Download Checklist PDF

Graduate Requirements

For master's and doctoral program applicants.

Bachelor's degree from accredited institution

Minimum GPA of 3.2 (on 4.0 scale)

GRE scores (varies by program)

Statement of purpose (750-1000 words)

Three letters of recommendation

Resume or curriculum vitae

Download Checklist PDF

International Student Requirements

Additional requirements for students applying from outside the United States.

All undergraduate or graduate requirements above

English proficiency test (TOEFL, IELTS, or Duolingo English Test)

International credential evaluation for non-U.S. academic records

Financial documentation showing ability to cover tuition and living expenses

Copy of passport identification page
