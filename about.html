Pioneering a new era of technology education and innovation since 2005. Learn about our mission, values, and the people who make BATU exceptional.

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To empower the next generation of technologists, innovators, and leaders through cutting-edge education, interdisciplinary research, and a collaborative environment that fosters creativity and problem-solving.

Our Vision

To become the global epicenter for technological advancement and digital transformation, where groundbreaking ideas evolve into solutions that positively impact society and shape a more equitable, sustainable future.

Guiding Principles

Our Core Values

Innovation

We embrace cutting-edge technologies and methodologies, constantly pushing boundaries to create new knowledge and solutions.

Collaboration

Integrity

We uphold the highest ethical standards in research, teaching, and all institutional activities.

Global Impact

We strive to develop technologies and solutions that address pressing global challenges and improve lives worldwide.

Our Journey

BATU Through the Years

From our humble beginnings to becoming a global leader in technology education and innovation.

2005

BATU University was founded with a vision to create a new kind of institution focused on emerging technologies and interdisciplinary research.

2008

Established the Center for Advanced Computing, our first research center dedicated to high-performance computing and algorithm development.

2012

Launched our flagship Artificial Intelligence program, one of the first comprehensive AI degrees in the country.

2015

Opened the Innovation Hub, a state-of-the-art facility designed to foster collaboration between students, faculty, and industry partners.

2018

Recognized as a Top 20 Technology University globally, with groundbreaking research in machine learning and quantum computing.

2021

Implemented our Smart Campus initiative, integrating IoT technologies throughout the university to enhance student experience and operational efficiency.

2024

Celebrating 20 years of innovation with the launch of the Global Technology Institute, focusing on solving grand challenges through collaborative research.
