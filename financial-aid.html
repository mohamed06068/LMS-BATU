Get Started

How to Apply for Financial Aid

Follow these steps to access financial support for your education at BATU.

1

Complete the FAFSA

Submit the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) to determine your eligibility for federal and institutional aid. Use BATU's school code: 012345.

2

Apply for Scholarships

Complete BATU's scholarship application form, including essays and recommendation letters, to be considered for merit-based awards.

3

Submit Additional Documents

Provide any required documents, such as tax returns, proof of income, or verification forms, to complete your financial aid profile.

4

Review Award Letter

Receive and review your financial aid award letter, detailing grants, scholarships, loans, and work-study opportunities.

5

Accept Aid Offer

Log into your student portal to accept or decline the financial aid package. Ensure you understand the terms of any loans.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

The Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) determines your eligibility for federal and institutional financial aid, including grants, loans, and work-study programs. It’s crucial for accessing need-based aid at BATU.

International students are not eligible for federal aid but can apply for BATU’s merit-based scholarships and external scholarships. Contact our International Office for more details.

Maintain Satisfactory Academic Progress (SAP), which includes a minimum GPA and completion rate. You must also remain enrolled in a degree program and submit renewal applications annually.

Financial aid award letters are typically sent out by April 1, 2026, for students who meet the February 1 priority deadline.
